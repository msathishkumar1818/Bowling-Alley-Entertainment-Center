★ Our 38-Year Evolutionary Heritage

From Solid Maple Roots to The Cosmic Future

What began in 1988 as a 12-lane neighborhood alley has evolved through relentless craftsmanship and architectural innovation into a 45,000 sq.ft. multi-sensory leisure sanctuary.

1988 The Genesis

The Solid Maple Era

Founded with 12 authentic maple wood lanes and hand-cranked pinsetters, quickly becoming the regional heartbeat for competitive league bowlers.

✓ 12 Original Hardwood Lanes
2012 Sensory Leap

The Cosmic Revolution

Pioneered UV neon cosmic bowling in the region with 50,000-watt nightclub audio rigs, glowing pins, and choreographed laser canopy light shows.

✓ 50kW Concert Audio & UV
