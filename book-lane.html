Equipment Standards

The Professional Fitting & Valet Guide

Bowling performance starts with balance and slide stability. All rental footwear is treated with dual-action antimicrobial UV-C chambers between each guest use.

Shoe Sizing Protocol

We stock sizes from US Toddler 7 up to Adult Men 16. Rental shoes feature genuine sliding microfiber soles on both feet for ambidextrous play. Clean athletic socks are required.

The 10% Body Weight Ball Rule

As a standard rule, select a bowling ball that weighs approximately 10% of your body weight (e.g. 140 lbs = 14 lb ball), up to the maximum 16 lbs. For youth bowlers, 6 to 9 lb balls ensure safe, strain-free deliveries.

Good to Know Before Rolling

Arrival Protocol & Booking FAQs

Everything you need to guarantee a seamless matchday arrival.

We strongly advise arriving 15 minutes prior to your reservation time. This allows your squad to check in, receive properly fitted shoes at the valet, and choose your favorite ball weights without cutting into your lane play clock.
Official bowling shoes must be worn on the approach at all times to prevent damage to the lane wood and ensure safe sliding. Socks are strictly required. If someone in your party forgets socks, new sanitized cotton pairs are available at the counter for $3.00.
Store-bought birthday cakes or cupcakes are warmly permitted with all reserved birthday and party bookings! However, other outside food, fast food, and outside alcoholic beverages are strictly prohibited due to health department guidelines. Our full kitchen bistro and taphouse are open for all orders.
Online lane bookings can be rescheduled or canceled for a full store credit or date transfer up to 4 hours before your reservation time through our self-service Member Portal or by giving us a call.
